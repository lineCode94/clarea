## Important distinctions

- The Travel Kit contains oil 30 ml, foam 20 ml, toner 30 ml, ampoule 30 ml and soothing cream 30 ml. It contains no sunscreen. Do not apply the original ampoule's ingredient claim to the whole kit.
- Tea-Trica B5 is a 75 ml cream, not the Tea-Trica cleanser. Its published formula includes tea tree oil; do not claim it is essential-oil-free.
- Poremizing toner is an exfoliant with four acid families, not just a hydrating toner. Do not claim permanent pore closure or acne treatment.
- Air-Fit Light is SPF30, not Air-Fit Plus SPF50. No water-resistance claim has been verified for either sunscreen.
- The current original ampoule listing includes water, glycerin, butylene glycol, Centella Asiatica extract, 1,2-hexanediol, cellulose gum and ethylhexylglycerin. Older online lists differ. Do not assert 100% Centella extract without checking the owner's actual INCI label.
- The website explicitly labels ingredient summaries as selected/key ingredients, not complete INCI lists. For allergy checking, request photographs of each back label. Stock photos cannot establish the exact formula/batch in hand.
- [AAD sunscreen guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/shade-clothing-sunscreen/how-to-use-stick-spray-sunscreens): adequate multi-pass stick coverage and reapplication outdoors. General safety guidance is distinguished from manufacturer product claims.

No halal certification, pregnancy-safety guarantee or medical-treatment claims were added.
